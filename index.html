Kept in this browser, on this device. No account, no server, no sync, no analytics. Clearing browser data deletes it.

an entry
One line you wrote, and when. Rocky keeps the line for you. It does not read it.
quiet
The time between one entry and the next. Treated as information, not as missing data.
your usual gap
The average quiet across your own record. Yours only, never compared to anyone.
the multiple, written 3.4x
The current quiet divided by your usual gap. A ratio, not a score, not a risk level, not a measure of how you are doing.
what you decided
A sentence you wrote in advance, handed back when your condition is met. Rocky holds it and does not write one.
  • Know how you feel. No way to tell, and it does not guess.
  • Read what you write. Only a count of how many people you named.
  • Compare you to anyone. Nothing here knows what anyone else does.
  • Diagnose, score, or rate you.
  • Detect a crisis. It cannot, and does not try.
  • Decide anything you did not decide first.
  • Send anything anywhere. No network access at all.
  • Talk to you. No voice, no opinions. It hands back your own words and stops.

A list of limits, not of features.

None of the ideas here are original. Three are worth knowing, for what they support and where they stop.

  • Deciding in advance, in if-then form. Well studied, with a medium to large effect on whether the intended thing happens. Effects were larger with if-then phrasing and with rehearsal, which is why the sentence has this shape and is shown back on ordinary days.
  • Naming people in advance. One component of safety planning, which has solid clinical evidence. Rocky is not a safety plan and that evidence does not transfer. A safety plan is made with a clinician and covers warning signs and access to means. If that is what you need, ask a clinician for one rather than using this.
  • Writing things down. Studied for decades, small to moderate benefits, using long reflective sessions. A one line entry is not that, so treat this as a record and not as a treatment.

Rocky has not been studied. The above is evidence for the ideas it borrows, which is a weaker claim.
